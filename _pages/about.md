Hello! I'm a Postdoctoral Researcher at the [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://www.gatech.edu/), working with [Prof. Sehoon Ha](https://faculty.cc.gatech.edu/~sha9/). I received my Ph.D. in Mechanical Science and Engineering from the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://mechse.illinois.edu/) under [Prof. Joao Ramos](https://publish.illinois.edu/jlramos/), and my M.S. in Robotics from [KAIST](https://www.kaist.ac.kr/en/) under [Prof. Dong-soo Kwon](http://robot.kaist.ac.kr/professor/?ckattempt=1). My undergraduate degree in Robotics is from [Kwangwoon University](https://www.kw.ac.kr/en/univ/electronic08_1.jsp#:~:text=Kwangwoon%20University's%20Division%20of%20Robotics%20is%20also%20the%20home%20of,applied%20to%20various%20industrial%20fields.), advised by [Prof. Juhoon Back](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=b6GLVA8AAAAJ&hl=en). I also worked as a research engineer at [ROEN Surgical Inc.](https://www.roensurgical.com/), a medical robot startup.

**Goal:** Build a robot like Baymax—one that steps into danger zones so humans don't have to, and lives alongside us in everyday life.

**Research Interest:** Model-based and learning-based control, humanoid robots, state estimation, and embodied AI.
